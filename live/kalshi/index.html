How to read it same method as the research notebooks

Every probability is the midpoint of the yes bid and ask on an open Kalshi contract (last trade only where the notebook allows a fallback). Threshold ladders (“CPI ≥ 3.0%”, “10Y ≥ 4.8%”) are read as survival curves and forced monotone with a pool-adjacent-violators fit; the size of that repair is disclosed on every card as a data-quality flag, together with median bid–ask spread and volume. Medians are interpolated from the fitted curve. Exact-outcome and range markets are normalised to sum to one and the raw sum is shown. Nothing is averaged into a “master probability”: the labour lens and the growth ladder display their components side by side, because disagreement between contracts is information. History lines are completed daily candles of one persistent contract, never stitched across events.
